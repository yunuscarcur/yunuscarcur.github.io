Watchly: What To Watch

A movie and TV tracking Android app live on Google Play that simplifies discovery and enables shared watchlists with friends.

Role
Solo developer
Platform
Android · Play Store
Scope
188 Kotlin files · 8 languages
Year
2025 – 2026

Problem

Browsing multiple streaming platforms to decide what to watch and keeping a shared watchlist with friends is usually fragmented. I wanted to build a fast, multilingual Android app that unifies discovery and collaborative lists in one place.

There were two critical engineering constraints: keeping the TMDB API key out of the client APK, and loading dozens of title details inside a watchlist with low latency instead of making sequential client calls.

My Role

I built the Android client, Node.js serverless backend, and web sharing pages solo. I designed the UI in Jetpack Compose and localized the app into 8 languages: Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, and Portuguese.

On the backend I authored 13 Cloud Functions covering a batched TMDB proxy, push notifications when friends update shared lists, invite/follow deep-link resolution, list cover uploads, member stats, and Google Play compliant account deletion. I enforced Detekt and Spotless (ktlint) static checks and enabled R8 shrinking for release builds.

Architecture

1 · Android client Kotlin & Jetpack Compose 188 Kotlin files, Credential Manager Google Sign-In, Play In-App Updates, WorkManager notifications, and App Links.
2 · Serverless backend 13 Cloud Functions (europe-west1) Secret Manager-backed TMDB proxy handling up to 50 parallel calls per request, plus invite, follow, and notification triggers.
3 · Data & web sharing Realtime DB, Storage & Hosting Real-time shared list sync, uCrop + ExifInterface profile media, and /join, /sharedlist, /movie web redirects.

Technologies

Hardest technical challenge

When a user opened a watchlist containing dozens of movies or shows, firing individual TMDB requests from the client caused noticeable latency and risked exposing the API key inside the APK. At the same time, building a general proxy required preventing SSRF and abuse.

I implemented a tmdbProxy Cloud Function deployed in europe-west1. Every call verifies Firebase Auth and validates paths/parameters against strict regex rules, while redirect: 'manual' prevents leaking the Bearer token on 3xx redirects. Up to 50 sub-requests run in parallel via Promise.allSettled so a single failing title never breaks the batch, and maxInstances: 10 caps peak billing.

Outcome

Watchly is live on Google Play at v1.2.5 with 248 registered users and 39 shared watchlists actively used across 8 languages.
