İnönü University Campus Twin

A 3D campus digital twin built for our COP31 "AI-Assisted Green University Digital Twin" proposal, simulating 5-year campus budget, energy, and carbon emissions.

Event
COP31 Climate Summit
Support
YÖK supported
Platform
Flutter (Android & Web)
Year
2026

Problem

A university campus's electricity, natural gas, water, maintenance, and transport costs usually remain buried in static spreadsheets rather than being tangible for students and decision-makers.

Our goal was to model İnönü University across 6 campus zones in 3D and let users allocate a 5-year budget across buildings and infrastructure while seeing immediate cost, satisfaction, and carbon (tCO₂e) impact.

My Role

I contributed to the 50-file Flutter mobile & web application, the map-to-Flutter bridge (WebView on Android, same-origin iframe on web), TR/EN localization, PDF policy report export, and the English COP31 presentation video.

Architecture

1 · 3D map layer MapLibre + Blender & Python OSM and Blender (.glb) building models, ~56k trees extracted from satellite & Meta/WRI data, and NOAA solar angle lighting.
2 · Simulation core Pure Dart (unit tested) Framework-free lib/oyun/ engine modeling Malatya climate, facade wear, solar plant, electric shuttle, SDG/ESG, and GreenComp metrics.
3 · Client & reports Flutter Android & Web ikiz_kanali_mobil and ikiz_kanali_web bridges, bilingual TR/EN UI, and shareable PDF policy export via pdf_rapor.dart.

App screens

Technologies

Flutter Dart MapLibre Blender (.glb) Python WebView / iframe PDF Export OpenStreetMap

Hardest technical challenge

Rendering ~56,000 trees (~32k from Meta & WRI 1 m canopy height maps and ~24k detected from satellite imagery) alongside all campus buildings smoothly on both smartphones and 1440p/4K presentation displays was the toughest engineering hurdle.

We partitioned trees into 100 m cells and buildings into 200 m cells to cull out-of-view geometry, simplified distant buildings by ~60% fewer triangles, and rendered trees smaller than 16 px on screen as single-quad points. When frame rate drops below 24 fps, dynamic quality tiers (K1–K3) step down automatically and recover above 48 fps, paired with per-frame pixel caps for integrated and discrete GPUs.

Outcome

Supported by Turkey's Council of Higher Education (YÖK) and selected for presentation at the COP31 Climate Summit. A patent application is currently in preparation.
