IZeka

My first Android app built from scratch while learning Kotlin and Android Studio: an offline 3-stage educational game for children with custom audio, visuals, jokers, and token mechanics.

Role
Solo developer
Platform
Android (minSdk 24 – 35)
Scope
15 Kotlin files · 6 screens
Year
2024 – 2025

Problem

Right after starting university, I wanted to learn Android development by shipping a complete working game rather than small textbook exercises. I designed an offline app where kids identify animals and objects by sound, then reinforce learning through word puzzles and quizzes.

My Role

Beyond writing all of the code, I created every theme illustration and audio clip myself — implementing 6 Activities, 3 SQLite question database helpers, a background MusicService, and a PreferencesManager for scores and tokens.

Architecture

1 · 3 game stages 6 Activities (XML) Audio-visual object/animal recognition, word puzzles, and multiple-choice quiz screens.
2 · Local storage 3× SQLite DatabaseHelper Each stage stores its question bank in on-device SQLite tables for 100% offline play.
3 · Audio & rewards MusicService & PreferencesManager Seamless background music across screens, lifeline jokers, and an in-game token balance.

Technologies

Kotlin Android XML SQLite Android Service SharedPreferences

Outcome

This was the project that taught me Android lifecycles, local databases, background services, and UI layout. Looking back, I would consolidate the 3 separate DatabaseHelper classes and SecondActivity–SixthActivity naming into a unified architecture and clean package structure — lessons I applied directly a year later in Watchly with Jetpack Compose.
