Autonomous Irrigation System

An Arduino UNO Q project that evaluates soil and weather sensor readings per plant type to water only as much as needed, paired with a mobile companion app.

Problem

Timer-based irrigation systems run blindly regardless of humidity, wind, or already wet soil — wasting water or leaving plants under-watered.

Our goal was to build a system that decides based on live environmental readings and plant requirements while automatically protecting the pump when tank water runs low.

My Role

Within our team accepted into Robotistan's Arduino UNO Q program, I am working on the sensor architecture, AI-assisted decision flow, and mobile monitoring/control integration.

Architecture

1 · Sensor layer Environmental sensing Reads soil moisture, barometric pressure, air humidity, wind speed, and tank water level.
2 · Decision layer Arduino UNO Q + analysis Evaluates readings against plant profiles and activates the pump only when tank level is safe.
3 · Monitoring Mobile app Displays live sensor metrics, irrigation history, and remote manual override controls.

Technologies

Arduino UNO Q C++ Soil Moisture Sensor Pressure & Humidity Sensor Wind Sensor Water Level Sensor Mobile App

Outcome & current status

The proposal was selected among 100 projects in Robotistan's Arduino UNO Q program. The hardware prototype and field testing are currently underway; measured results and prototype photos will be added once testing concludes.
