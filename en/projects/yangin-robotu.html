Autonomous Fire-Fighting Robot

An autonomous Arduino robot that patrols while avoiding obstacles using three ultrasonic sensors, locates flame sources from three directions, and extinguishes them with an onboard fan.

Board
Arduino UNO
Language
C++ (Arduino IDE)
Sensors
3× HC-SR04 · 3× Y-026
Year
2025
Controller
Arduino UNO (C++)
Obstacle detection
3× HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance sensors (left, right, front)
Flame detection
3× Y-026 analog flame sensors (3-axis comparison)
Drive & suppression
Dual DC motors + L9110 fan module

Problem

I wanted to build an autonomous indoor prototype capable of patrolling without human intervention, navigating around obstacles, and immediately switching from patrol mode to flame targeting and suppression when fire is detected.

My Role

I wired the circuit, calibrated sensor thresholds across all 6 sensors, built the dual DC motor steering logic, and programmed the patrol / target / extinguish state machine in C++.

Architecture

1 · Patrol mode 3× HC-SR04 ultrasonic Continuously measures left, right, and front clearance and steers toward open paths when obstacles appear.
2 · Flame detection 3× Y-026 analog sensors Compares analog intensity across three directions and aligns the robot toward the strongest flame signal.
3 · Suppression L9110 fan module Stops at a safe distance, activates the fan, and resumes patrol once flame readings return to baseline.

Technologies

Arduino UNO C++ HC-SR04 Y-026 Flame Sensor L9110 Fan Module DC Motor Control

Outcome

The robot reliably navigated an indoor test arena, located small flame sources, and extinguished them autonomously. The multi-sensor calibration experience directly paved the way for our Robotistan UNO Q Autonomous Irrigation project.
