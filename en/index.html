About

I started studying Computer Engineering at İnönü University in 2024. I built my first Android app, IZeka, while learning Kotlin and XML — an educational game for children where I created everything from code to artwork and sound assets.

I then published Watchly on Google Play and began building websites and management panels for businesses under the name Tardus Studio.

Alongside software, I enjoy embedded hardware. I built an autonomous Arduino robot that avoids obstacles, locates flames, and extinguishes them, and I'm now working on an autonomous irrigation system in Robotistan's UNO Q program.

Most of my hands-on experience is in Android development with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, paired with Firebase on the backend.
